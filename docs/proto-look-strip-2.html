The Look — five more meters (no line)

Same busts; the "he is coming" meter is a drawn object instead of a bar. Drag / play. E ribbon stitched toward her · F his footsteps on the parquet · G her fan closing · H a taper burning down · I a sand-glass running out.

E · the ribbon

Sir William Lucas has seen you. He is making his way over.

A silk ribbon looped between them, like the cord of a dance card. The gold thread runs from his end toward hers; the wax seal at her side warms as he closes. Curve, not a bar.

F · footsteps

Sir William Lucas has seen you. He is making his way over.

Buckled-shoe prints appear on the parquet, one pair at a time, from his side toward hers; each glints once as it lands, then settles into the floor. The distance is literally his steps.

G · her fan

Sir William Lucas has seen you. He is making his way over.

A lace fan open at the bottom centre; its leaves fold one by one as he nears, until it snaps shut — the period signal that a conversation is about to begin. Nothing between the busts but the fan.

H · the taper

Sir William Lucas has seen you. He is making his way over.

A single candle beside his bust, burning down as he comes; the flame leans toward her and gutters at the end. Time, not distance — reads as a countdown without a bar.

I · the sand-glass

Sir William Lucas has seen you. He is making his way over.

A small brass sand-glass between them; the sand runs from the upper bulb into the lower with a thin falling thread. When the top is empty, he is at her elbow.
