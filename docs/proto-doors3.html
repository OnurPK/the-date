Intro — draw the oval, I do the 3D

The phone sits in the middle; the oval is the doors' floor track and may spill outside the phone. Drag the oval body to move it, drag the four handles to resize (width = radius, height = how flat the camera sees it). Doors stand on the track facing the camera, shrinking toward the far end; only the far half is shown.

How the 3D is derived — the oval is a circle of radius rx lying on the floor, tilted so it projects to what you drew (sin tilt = ry / rx), centred at (cx, cy). It is one real 3D scene: the doors are the faces of a regular polygon inscribed in that circle (side = 2·rx·tan(π/N)), so they meet edge to edge and bend around the curve with no seams. far scale sets the camera distance (how much the far side shrinks); slots = number of faces (auto slots picks the count that makes each face one door wide); arc = the visible part of the far side. Values are in phone pixels (390×844, origin top-left of the phone), so the JSON from copy values ports straight into the real intro.
