# Season 1 — "The Wit at Netherfield" (draft v0.1, for Onur's approval)

Maintained by the `pp-season` skill. Episodes 1–2 are shipped and are facts; 3–6 are proposals. Open decisions in §8.

## 1. Controlling idea

Arabella wins every room she enters by treating it as a game, and the season ends when the one man who refuses to play makes her show a card she cannot take back.

## 2. Arabella's arc

- **Desire (conscious):** to master the county's marriage market on her own terms: choose, not be chosen; be priced by no one.
- **Misbelief:** that her heart is one more card she can hold or fold at will ("naming it is for another night", ep 1 coda).
- **Pressure per episode:** ep 1 the game turns real on the dance floor; ep 2 a man with no game (Ashbourne) makes the game look small; ep 3 Charlotte shows what "winning safely" costs; ep 4 Wickham shows what a player looks like from outside; ep 5 Pemberley shows the man behind the locked door; ep 6 the misbelief breaks: she is asked plainly and has no move left but the truth.
- **Break beat:** Rosings, ep 6, the interrupted confession becomes a question she must answer without wit.
