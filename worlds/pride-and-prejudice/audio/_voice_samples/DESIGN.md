# Voice design — the Assembly cast (3 samples each, one per "package")

Each character gets three designs, aligned to three coherent packages so a full cast can be picked in one go:

- **A · Period drama** — BBC costume-drama register: RP, measured, understated, warm room tone.
- **B · Intimate** — closer, softer, more modern-sounding delivery; less "performed", more like someone speaking near the listener.
- **C · Theatrical** — bigger, more characterful, stage-trained; wider dynamics, more colour.

Sample text = real lines from the-assembly (100–1000 chars). Previews are saved as `_voice_samples/<folder>/<A|B|C>_<n>.mp3` with `samples.json` holding the `generated_voice_id` needed to turn a preview into a saved voice (`/api/author/voice-create`).
