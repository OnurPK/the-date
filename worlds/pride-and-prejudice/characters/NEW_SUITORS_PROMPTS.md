# Pride & Prejudice — 8 New Suitors · künye + image prompts

Yeni damat adayları (roles.ai suitors ekranı için). Kanon karakterlere ek, film/kitap dünyasına uygun yeni yüzler.
**5 zengin + 3 fakir** (fakirlerden biri çocukluk aşkı).

Görseller mevcut figürlerle aynı stile göre yazıldı: **painterly-photoreal Regency portre**, tek erkek,
diz/bel üstü (three-quarter), dönem kıyafeti, sıcak dramatik ışık, dönem iç mekânı arkada.
Kart 2:3 olduğu için **`--ar 2:3`** kullan.

Her prompt formatı: **`[FIGSTYLE] + <karakter tarifi> + <mekân> + --ar 2:3 + [NEG]`**

## [FIGSTYLE]
> painterly-photoreal character portrait, Regency England early 1800s, a single young gentleman,
> three-quarter length standing pose, body angled slightly, historically accurate period dress,
> dramatic warm directional lighting, rich fabric and texture detail, romantic cinematic mood,
> shallow depth of field, elegant and tasteful, oil-painting-meets-photoreal finish, highly detailed face

## [NEG]
> text, watermark, modern objects, extra limbs, distorted hands, distorted face, cartoonish,
> anachronistic clothing, multiple people, blurry, lowres

> **İpucu:** kartta figürü location arka planı üstüne bindirmek istersen prompt sonuna
> `, isolated on plain neutral background, subject cut-out` ekleyip [NEG]'e `, busy background` koy —
> böylece şeffaf/temiz zeminli figür alırsın ve arkaya kendi mekân görselini koyabilirsin.

# ZENGİN ADAYLAR (5)

## 1. Lord Sebastian Ravenscar — *"The Dark Viscount"*
- **ID:** `lord_ravenscar`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A viscount of a vast northern estate — old title, darker fortune; wealth that whispers of gambling tables and duels.
- **Traits:** Magnetic · Reckless
- **Bio:** All danger and beautiful manners. To be seen on his arm is a triumph — and a scandal waiting to happen.
- **Servet (narrative):** Immense but rumoured half-mortgaged to his vices.
- **Status önerisi:** Available · **Scandal riski yüksek** (Allure ↑↑, Scandal ↑)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a strikingly handsome dark-haired young viscount, sharp jaw, brooding grey eyes, a
faint scar, black tailcoat with a deep-wine silk waistcoat, cravat loosened just enough to read as
dangerous, one hand resting on a cane; behind him a shadowed candlelit gaming room, velvet drapes,
low amber light — seductive and perilous. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 2. Sir Henry Ashbourne — *"The Safe Harbour"*
- **ID:** `sir_ashbourne`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A baronet of a well-kept country seat — comfortable, unencumbered, respectable to the bone.
- **Traits:** Steady · Kind
- **Bio:** A widower in his prime; sensible, generous, a little grave. The match every sensible mother prays for.
- **Servet (narrative):** Solidly wealthy, debt-free, land that pays.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (the reliable option)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a warm, distinguished gentleman of about forty, chestnut hair greying at the temples,
gentle intelligent eyes, a well-cut bottle-green coat and buff breeches, calm assured posture, a
faint kind smile; behind him a sunlit country-house library, tall windows, worn leather books —
steady, safe, dependable. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 3. Captain Frederick Vane — *"The Self-Made Sailor"*
- **ID:** `capt_vane`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A naval captain returned from the wars rich on prize-money — no old name, but a fortune won by his own hand.
- **Traits:** Bold · Proud (of his own making)
- **Bio:** Sun-weathered, quick to laugh, quicker to act. New money and no shame in it — which the drawing rooms have not forgiven.
- **Servet (narrative):** Newly, dazzlingly rich; earned, not inherited.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (new-money stigma — Allure ↑, mild Scandal)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a rugged confident naval captain in his early thirties, sun-bronzed skin, tousled
dark-blond hair, keen blue eyes, dark-blue Royal Navy uniform with gold epaulettes and brass
buttons, an easy weathered grin, arms loosely crossed; behind him a harbour at golden hour, ship's
rigging and masts soft in the distance — bold, self-made, magnetic. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 4. Monsieur Rafael Devereux — *"The Émigré"*
- **ID:** `mr_devereux`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A French aristocrat in exile — a comte without his lands, living on a rescued fortune and impeccable charm.
- **Traits:** Charming · Enigmatic
- **Bio:** Cosmopolitan, cultured, dangerously fluent in flattery. No one is quite sure how much of him is true.
- **Servet (narrative):** Rich, but exiled — wealth that may be smaller than it dazzles.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (foreign suspicion — Allure ↑, Scandal risk)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` an elegant continental gentleman, olive skin, dark wavy hair, expressive dark eyes,
a beautifully tailored plum-and-silver French-cut coat, lace at the cuffs, a signet ring, poised
theatrical grace, a knowing half-smile; behind him a candlelit salon with gilt mirrors and a
harpsichord — cultured, charming, unreadable. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 5. Mr Augustus Hale — *"New Money"*
- **ID:** `mr_hale`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** Heir to a great manufacturing fortune from the north — no coat of arms, but more ready money than half the peerage.
- **Traits:** Ambitious · Blunt
- **Bio:** Modern, restless, unimpressed by titles. Buys what old families merely inherit — and knows it grates on them.
- **Servet (narrative):** Enormously rich in trade; socially looked-down-upon for it.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (trade stigma — Scandal-adjacent)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a robust, sharp-eyed young man of new wealth, close-cropped auburn hair, strong
features, a slightly-too-fine dark plum coat and heavy gold watch-chain, an assured almost-defiant
stance; behind him a grand but newly-furnished drawing room, everything gleaming and just-bought —
ambitious, modern, self-made. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

# FAKİR ADAYLAR (3)

## 6. Mr Thomas Fenwick — *"The Childhood Love"* ⭐
- **ID:** `mr_fenwick`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A country curate's son — no land, no fortune, only a modest living and an old, old promise.
- **Traits:** Devoted · Earnest
- **Bio:** The boy from next door, grown gentle and true. He has nothing to offer but a whole heart — and the memory of who you were before the world got complicated.
- **Servet (narrative):** Poor; a small curacy, a cottage, a garden.
- **Status önerisi:** Available · **çocukluk aşkı** (Affection başlangıcı yüksek, Desire düşük)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a gentle handsome young man of modest means, soft brown hair, warm hazel eyes, an
open honest face, a plain but neat dark coat slightly worn at the cuffs, no jewels, a shy tender
smile; behind him a sunlit country lane, a hedgerow and a distant church steeple, wildflowers —
humble, sincere, first-love warmth. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 7. Ensign Edward Pryce — *"The Idealist Officer"*
- **ID:** `ens_pryce`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A junior militia ensign of good manners but no money — his commission bought on hope and a widowed mother's savings.
- **Traits:** Brave · Naïve
- **Bio:** Young, ardent, full of honour and empty of pounds. He would fight a duel for your glove and starve for your smile.
- **Servet (narrative):** Poor; an ensign's meagre pay, no prospects yet.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (poor but gallant)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a fresh-faced young militia officer, early twenties, neat sandy hair, bright earnest
eyes, a scarlet regimental coat that is clearly his best and only fine thing, straight-backed
eager posture; behind him a village green at dusk, a militia tent and a fading sunset — young,
idealistic, gallant. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`

## 8. Mr Jasper Quill — *"The Penniless Poet"*
- **ID:** `mr_quill`
- **Rütbe/Hane:** A gentleman by birth, a pauper by choice — traded an inheritance for ink, verse, and a garret.
- **Traits:** Passionate · Impractical
- **Bio:** Beautiful, brilliant, and utterly broke. He will write you into immortality and forget to pay the rent.
- **Servet (narrative):** Poor; lives on unsold verses and the kindness of friends.
- **Status önerisi:** Available (romantic ruin — Allure ↑, no fortune)

**Prompt:**
`[FIGSTYLE] +` a lean romantic young poet, tousled dark curls, pale intense features, ink-stained
fingers, a rumpled loosely-tied cravat and a threadbare but artful coat, a faraway burning gaze
holding a small book; behind him a candlelit attic study, scattered papers, a rain-streaked
window — passionate, bohemian, beautifully poor. `--ar 2:3 + [NEG]`
