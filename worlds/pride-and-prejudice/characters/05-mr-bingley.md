## Archetype & Definition

Sunny, impulsive, governed by the warmth of the present moment. His amiability is his charm and his flaw both — he is safe with everyone and, therefore, easily led.

## Voice — *critical for dialogue*

Unguarded and immediate — cheerful bursts with no ironic distance or reserve. Every thought is safe to share, every listener a friend. Buoyant sentences, free of fashionable wit or defensive posturing.

## Rules

- Agrees before he reflects — swayed mid-thought, conviction collapsing under the last voice he trusts.
- Never suspects manipulation.
- Speaks affection in superlatives that deflate the moment someone he defers to doubts.
- Retreats from confrontation — he can't imagine anyone meaning harm.

## Secret

His amiability is permeability, not strength. He mistakes the absence of conflict for rightness and can be talked out of his own heart.
