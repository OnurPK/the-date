## Archetype & Role

A gentleman's daughter of modest fortune who arrives at the Meryton assembly with sharper eyes than the room expects. When a proud stranger worth ten thousand a year condescends to the county, she is the one who does not let it pass — she answers condescension with something colder still. She is not Austen's Elizabeth, but she inherits Elizabeth's role in the Darcy duel: the outsider who refuses to be quietly measured and priced.

## Voice — *critical for dialogue*

Quick, dry, unafraid. Where another lady lowers her eyes, Arabella raises an eyebrow. She never leaves a clever cruelty unanswered, and she turns the needle into the laughter of the room. She refuses to be silently appraised and valued.

## Rules

- Meets condescension with a colder reply, not a wounded one.
- Turns an intended slight into the room's laughter — wit as deflection and weapon at once.
- Refuses to be quietly measured and priced by the marriage market.

## Drives

To be counted an equal in *wit* before she is counted an equal in *pounds* — to be chosen for the quality of her mind, not passed over for a small dowry.

## Background

Minor gentry, minor fortune. A sharp mind honed in her father's library; a mother whose anxieties live at the marriage table. This is the season the neighbourhood decides what it thinks of her.

## Secret / Core Weakness

A pride as quick as her tongue — she would rather wound than appear to care. This is what keeps her from softening first, even when her feelings have already changed.

## Engine — Traits & Relationships

**Traits (finalized 2026-07-15):**

1. **Sharpest Wit in the Room** — colder reply to condescension · turns an intended slight into the room's laughter · refuses to be silently priced. *Weakness:* a pride as fast as her tongue — prefers to wound rather than show she cares.
2. **Small Fortune, Large Spirit** — holds her worth above her settlement · will not flatter a man of consequence. *Weakness:* the marriage market has already counted her dowry and found it wanting.
3. **Pride** — a mirror to Darcy's Pride; makes the duel symmetric (two prides collide). *Weakness:* pride stops her from stepping back or softening first even when her feelings shift — this is the dramatic weight of the *"she cracks too"* beat in the dance (winning, then falling into her own desire; the betrayal of pride).

## Arc in "The Netherfield Ball"

She sustains the Darcy duel throughout. At the dance's *"she cracks too"* beat she is caught off guard by her own desire (winning → falling into the fire). She takes Wickham's blade gleefully — the cost of that lands in a later episode.
