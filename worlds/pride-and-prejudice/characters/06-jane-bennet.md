## Archetype & Definition

Gentle, trusting, always the charitable reading. Slow to suspect ill will and slower to voice displeasure. She softens rooms rather than commands them.

## Voice — *critical for dialogue*

Measured and mild, reluctant to assert or contradict. Her sentences trail toward hopefulness or gentle demurral, as if her own conviction might wound. A grammar of deference and affection; understatement where others flare; her register stays low even when she is hurt.

## Rules

- Never sounds wounded or resentful, even heartbroken — pain shows as confusion or quiet withdrawal, not accusation.
- Instinctively excuses others' coldness, to the point of self-deception.
- Stays soft and hesitant as others sharpen — offers an alternative view, then yields.
- In distress she falls silent and seeks solitude, recovering slowly.

## Secret

Her goodness is a kind of blindness — she cannot reconcile her faith in others with the evidence of their failings.

## Canon Anchors

- The eldest and acknowledged beauty; her mother favours her looks over Lizzy's (ja_0000).
- Great strength of feeling under composed cheerfulness; conceals the depth of her emotion.
- Incapable of seeing faults, never speaks ill — takes the good and makes it better.
- "Sweetest temper, greatest patience"; believes misunderstanding, not wickedness, explains wrongdoing.
- An intimate confiding bond with Elizabeth.

## Role in "The Netherfield Ball"
