## Status — read this first

The playable character **Arabella Frost** inherits Elizabeth's *shape* in the Darcy duel, so Elizabeth herself is deliberately kept in the background of this cut. Onur's ruling: she is on stage, but as texture — a Bennet daughter, Jane's sister, Charlotte's old friend — and she carries **no** duel or dialogue load of her own.

## Presence in "The Netherfield Ball"

- On stage as background / mingle texture; a recognisable Bennet among the guests.
- **Does not** carry the romantic Darcy duel — that is Arabella's, and the episode keeps a single duel centre.

## Separation rules (to prevent overlap with Arabella)

- **Collins:** courts a *cousin* for the entail; since Arabella is an outsider (not a Bennet), Collins's marriage-suit naturally aims at **Elizabeth** in the background — a comic thread the player watches.
- Do **not** give Elizabeth a separate duel or dialogue load — atmosphere only, plus the two links above (Charlotte's friend, Collins's target).
