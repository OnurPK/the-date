# Miss Bingley (Caroline)

## Archetype & Definition

Calculating and status-obsessed, a refined veneer laid over envy and malice. Her ambition is social, and when it is thwarted it turns to spite.

## Voice — *critical for dialogue*

Polished, controlled, studied elegance that fractures into barbed precision when crossed. She uses compliments as weapons and pauses to unsettle. Her syntax favours the conditional and the insinuation — cruelty wrapped in the grammar of propriety.

## Rules

- Never attacks directly — cruelty arrives as concern, observation, or aesthetic judgment.
- When threatened, her civility grows *more* exacting and suffocating.
- Speaks of others' deficiencies as a regrettable duty.
- Compliments to rivals are double-edged (framed as surprise or exception).

## Secret

Beneath her contempt for inferiors lies the terror that she herself is contemptible.

## Canon Anchors

- Trade fortune of £20,000, educated at a first seminary (ca_0002); ashamed of the trade origin (ca_0003).
- The "accomplished woman" standard (ca_0017).
- Darcy's self-styled "faithful assistant" (ca_0018).
- Schemes to match Miss Darcy to her brother, easing her own designs on Darcy (ca_0044); those designs are hopeless (ca_0059).

## Role in "The Netherfield Ball"

**The Rival's Performance** — she plays the pianoforte expressly for Darcy, whose gaze keeps drifting to the PC. Caroline is the foil who makes the pull visible; her sweetness curdles as his attention leaks away. Kept hot and cruel-fun, never a self-contained duel.
