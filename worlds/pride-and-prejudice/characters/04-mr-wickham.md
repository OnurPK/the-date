## Archetype & Definition

Charm as a full-body performance. Everything on the surface is warm and open; underneath is mercenary calculation and no loyalty.

## Voice — *critical for dialogue*

Smooth, confiding, a practiced ease that invites trust. He modulates between light flirtation and earnest disclosure, always calibrated to his audience. He speaks as a man unburdened by caution or shame, as though candour itself were proof of virtue.

## Rules

- Rehearsed sincerity — never stumbles.
- Keeps grievances *light* and agreeable (pain becomes another chance to connect).
- Shifts affection and attention without any visible transition.
- Never acknowledges calculation — every choice is framed as impulse, circumstance, or others' fault.

## Secret

His amiability is a performance sustained only as long as it serves him. No loyalty, no remorse — only the irritation of thwarted ambition.

## Role in "The Netherfield Ball"

**The Officer's Blade** — he is the messenger, not a flirtation. He hands the PC the story that Darcy cheated him of his inheritance. The pleasure is the *blade* (the anticipation of drawing it on Darcy), not Wickham himself. Kept short and pointed at Darcy; across the room Darcy watches and his jaw tightens (doubling as jealousy-bait). Do **not** let it bloom into courtship — the lie is a weapon she takes gleefully, and its cost lands later.

> **Canon note:** In the novel Wickham deliberately *avoids* the Netherfield ball to steer clear of Darcy. Placing him in the ballroom here is an intentional deviation so the player can take the blade. Flagged for confirmation with Onur; for now the JSON stands.
